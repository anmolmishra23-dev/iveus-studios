# Photography website research and design rationale

Reviewed 20 September 2026. Direction revised to reflect the studio's main work: model portfolios, brand photography and product shoots. Weddings remain a secondary category.

## References

- [Impresio Studio](https://www.theimpressio.in/): the supplied reference helped establish an approachable enquiry experience. Its family/maternity focus is not used as Iveus's main positioning.
- [House On The Clouds — Films](https://www.houseontheclouds.com/hotc-films): story-led visual presentation informed the cinematic pacing and generous images. No films or images were copied.
- [Nick Knight](https://www.nickknight.com/): reviewed after the commercial brief was clarified. Its archive brings fashion photographs and campaign imagery to the foreground. This informed the shift to a bold model-led hero, concise project labels and image-first commercial collections.
- [Samm Blake's wedding portfolio](https://www.sammblakeweddings.com/): editorial, image-led presentation informed the alternating image proportions and restrained navigation.
- [The Wedding Story](https://www.theweddingstory.com/): initially researched for the supplied wedding reference; this influence is now limited to the secondary wedding category.

These sites are creative references. No competitor photographs, branding, videos or copy are used in the delivered website.

## Direction applied to Iveus Studios

“Made to be seen” positions the studio around model portfolios, brand campaigns and product imagery. The supplied black-and-gold logo anchors the colour palette. Oversized editorial type sits over a fashion photograph, followed by a gallery filtered by Models, Brands, Products and Weddings. The brief form asks what the client is creating and defaults to Model portfolio.

The Three.js layer adds restrained pointer parallax and ambient light to the hero photograph. It does not hijack scrolling. Paper-coloured portfolio sections let skin tones, product textures and campaign colours stand apart from the charcoal background.
